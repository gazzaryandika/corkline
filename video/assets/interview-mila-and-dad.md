# Interview 3
Mila (9) and her dad
Home visit, Utrecht

Mila: "When I pay for my ice cream myself, I count the coins really carefully."
Mila: "I keep my birthday money in a glass jar, so I can see it getting bigger."
Dad: "She only understood saving when she bought her own skateboard, not when I explained it."
Dad: "In my banking app it isn't real money to her, it's just a number on my phone."

Interviewer: What happens when she wants something she can't afford yet?
Dad: She asks if she can do jobs around the house, and she keeps a list on the fridge.
Mila: "The list is better than the app because I can tick things off."
