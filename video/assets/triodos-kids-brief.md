# Triodos Kids: project brief

As cash disappears, children aged 6 to 12 are losing touch with what money is and where it goes.
Triodos assumes that families want a kids' bank product that also teaches sustainable money habits.
Parents feel responsible for teaching their children about money, but have no natural, everyday moment to do it.
Research so far: 4 child interviews, 3 parent interviews, 5 teacher interviews and an analysis of 121 Reddit threads.

Client: Triodos Bank. Course: TU Delft IDEM3210.
Deliverable: a concept for a kids' bank card, a physical companion object and a family app.
